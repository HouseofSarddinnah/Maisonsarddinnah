Welcome to Osaka

Osaka is Japan's kitchen and its soul — a city where the best food in the country meets some of its most welcoming people. From the neon-lit streets of Dotonbori to the serene grounds of Osaka Castle, from world-class luxury hotels to hidden izakayas, Osaka offers a different side of Japan that's equally compelling as Kyoto but distinctly more energetic. Whether you're here for the food, the family-friendly attractions, or the luxury shopping, Osaka delivers an experience that's both sophisticated and unpretentious.

This hub brings together all our Osaka guides — from luxury hotel recommendations to family travel tips — so you can plan your perfect Osaka journey.
