MAISON SARDDINNAH
LANGUAGE CHEAT SHEET FOR SOLO FEMALE TRAVELERS

JAPANESE
Sumimasen - Excuse me / I am sorry.
Arigatou gozaimasu - Thank you very much.
Eki wa doko desu ka? - Where is the station?
Kore wa nan desu ka? - What is this?
Tasukete kudasai - Please help me.

SPANISH
Disculpe - Excuse me.
Cuanto cuesta? - How much does it cost?
La cuenta, por favor - The bill, please.
Puede ayudarme? - Can you help me?
Necesito un taxi seguro - I need a safe taxi.

TRAVEL NOTE
Save your accommodation address in the local language before leaving. Keep this sheet offline and use a translation app when a conversation needs more precision.
