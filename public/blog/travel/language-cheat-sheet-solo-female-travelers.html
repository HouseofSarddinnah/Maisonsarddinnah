A Language Cheat Sheet for Solo Female Travelers

Small phrases that make everyday travel safer, warmer and less intimidating in Japan and Mexico.

You do not need fluency to travel respectfully. A few carefully learned phrases can change the tone of a practical exchange and help you ask clearly for what you need.

Japanese Essentials

  • Sumimasen: Excuse me / I am sorry.
  • Arigatou gozaimasu: Thank you very much.
  • Eki wa doko desu ka? Where is the station?
  • Tasukete kudasai: Please help me.

Spanish Essentials

  • Disculpe: Excuse me.
  • ¿Cuánto cuesta? How much does it cost?
  • La cuenta, por favor: The bill, please.
  • ¿Puede ayudarme? Can you help me?
  • Necesito un taxi seguro: I need a safe taxi.

Safety and Confidence

Save the phrases offline and write your accommodation address in the local language. Speak slowly, keep your boundaries clear and remember that using a translation app is not a failure. Preparedness is a form of freedom.
